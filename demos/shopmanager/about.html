ShopManager

A studio inventory ledger demonstrating CRUD operations, restocking workflows, and reporting with real-time updates and CSV export.

Features

Full CRUD Operations

Create, read, update, and delete products. Inline editing with immediate visual feedback.

Stock Level Tracking

Visual indicators for low stock and out-of-stock items. Automatic status calculation.

Reorder Queue

Track items that need reordering. Mark items as on reorder and receive stock with one click.

CSV Export

Export full inventory or filtered subsets to CSV for external analysis or backup.

Search & Filter

Quick search across product names. Filter by stock status: all, in stock, low, or reorder.

KPI Dashboard

Real-time KPIs showing total inventory value, units, low stock count, and items to reorder.
