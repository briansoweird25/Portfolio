Our story

Lumière started with a simple question: what if we made clothing that lasted decades instead of seasons?

We work with mills in Italy, Portugal, and Belgium — places where people have been making fabric for longer than fashion has had seasons. Every piece is cut in small runs, which means a little more time between order and arrival, but a lot less waste.

We're not interested in trends or collections that expire. We're interested in the coat you reach for every autumn, the shirt that works in every meeting, the trouser that somehow goes with everything you already own.

What guides us

Small runs

We produce in batches of 50–200 pieces. Most items are made after you order, which means less inventory waste and more thoughtful production.

Materials that matter

Italian wool, Belgian linen, Grade-A cashmere. We work with mills that have been perfecting their craft for generations, not chasing the lowest cost.

Built to repair

Every piece comes with care instructions and access to our repair service. Buttons, hems, tears — we'd rather fix it than sell you a new one.
